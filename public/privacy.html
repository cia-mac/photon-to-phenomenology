Privacy policy

Ciamac's Book of Illusions

The app collects nothing about you.

What the app collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no purchases. The app does not ask for your name, your location, your contacts, your photos, your camera or your microphone.

What stays on your device

The app remembers one setting, whether you have muted the narration, so it can start the way you left it. That setting is stored on your device and never leaves it.

Network

The app does not connect to the internet. Every illusion, and the narration that reads it aloud, is inside the app and works offline. Narration uses the speech voice built into your iPhone or iPad.

Third parties

There are none. No data is sent to anyone, because the app sends no data.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

The website

This page lives on photon.ciamac.com, a separate website that shows some of the same illusions in a browser. The website is not the app and has its own analytics. Nothing about the website is built into the app.

Changes

If the app ever collects anything, this page will say so before it does, and the new version of the app will say so in the App Store listing.
