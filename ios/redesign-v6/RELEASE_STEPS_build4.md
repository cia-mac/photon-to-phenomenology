# Releasing 1.0 (4): Cia's steps (2026-10-14)

Do the hardware checks on your phone first (build 4 is installed): VoiceOver through the home
screen, a piece, the guide, back; narration start, mute and changing pieces mid-line; largest text
and landscape on the home screen and one piece; a few pieces in airplane mode; haptics on a piece
with a reveal. If something is broken (an unreachable control, clipped navigation, a crash, dead
narration), do NOT release: tell me. A defect after release can only be fixed by a new review.

Then, in your signed-in Chrome, App Store Connect > Ciamac's Optical Illusions:
1. iOS App 1.0 page: status must read "Pending Developer Release". Build: 1.0 (4).
2. Look once: free price, the countries you want, Mac and Vision Pro availability off,
   screenshots in order (home screen first), blue icon, release option = manual.
3. Click "Release This Version" (top right) and confirm. Status moves to "Processing for
   Distribution", then "Ready for Distribution". The listing can take up to 24 hours to appear.
4. Tell me the status. When the listing shows, install the App Store copy and open a few pieces
   offline.

After 1.0 is Ready for Distribution, build 5 ships as version 1.0.1 (I change the version string).
