# What is current, and what draws ages 16 to 30: research for the illusions app (v1, 2026-10-11)

Sources: ChatGPT consult with web search (~/Developer/agent-notes/consults/20261011_173947.md); a cited web sweep by a helper agent (summarized here; items it read on the source page are marked fetched); Gemini consult not returned at time of writing. Trend rankings are judgment, not measured preference.

## Findings
1. There is no single "hip" look for this age range. What the award winners and loved apps share is immediate play plus a clearly authored identity. Apple Design Awards 2026 (fetched, apple.com/newsroom/2026/06): grug (scribbled, daily item), Moonlitt, Tide Guide (custom animation as the hero). 2025 (fetched, developer.apple.com/design/awards/2025): CapWords (stickers, real sounds), Balatro, finalists Denim, Vocabulary, Opal (custom haptics).
2. Liquid Glass (iOS 26) is the platform baseline for controls, with mixed reception. It will not make an app distinctive, and it must stay off the stimulus.
3. Dark ground, cream, Helvetica, hairlines now reads as the generic "premium minimal" template (both sources, judgment). This explains why two rounds of restraint looked lame.
4. Rising: tactile controls with sound and haptics ((Not Boring) apps); handmade or imperfect marks as a reaction to AI polish; expressive editorial type at poster scale (Tinder 2026 identity, Creative Review 2026-07-21); retro digital instruments (Poolsuite, Balatro, Nothing's dot matrix). Tired: mesh gradients, purple AI glow, interchangeable rounded cards.
5. First five seconds: content before explanation, one obvious gesture. For 21 pieces, a prominent playable exhibit plus a visual grid; an endless-feed imitation would expose the small inventory. Streaks need a reason to return; ChatGPT says reject them, the helper says a daily illusion is the top lever. Unresolved.
6. Illusions on short video: real reach but high variance (one Short at about 9.8M views, another at 1.8K). Winning format: instruction, contradiction, physical reveal, short explanation. News-summary evidence only for TikTok; no Reels or Shorts data found.
7. Competition: Unseeing (daily illusion, 70+, under 1,000 downloads, fetched mwm.ai); Optic Illusion (72 exhibits, 33 interactive, launched 2026-08-18); Imprint Illusions (4.3 from 85 ratings). "Interactive museum" is taken; none has a visual identity or a narrator.
8. Cringe: borrowed slang, brain emojis, fake IQ scores, "only 1% can see this", confetti without payoff (YPulse 2025-10-27; detail paywalled). Credible and cool: precise language, named phenomena, a recognizable human narrator, personality that comes from the interaction itself.
