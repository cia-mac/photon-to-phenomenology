Palmer, Vision Science §3.2 · the psychophysics of color

Raise each light. Where two overlap, a new color appears; where all three meet, white. None of it is in the light. Your eye makes it.

Emitted stimulus: the pixels, not a color name

The readout reports what the pixels actually emit: a linear-RGB radiance triple encoded to an sRGB hex code. The “you likely see” line is your visual system’s construction, not a property of the light. The red-plus-green overlap emits only red-primary and green-primary light, no yellow-wavelength photons; “yellow” is built by three cone signals (the L/M/S cone basis is Palmer §3.3). Turn a light off and its contribution vanishes from every overlap it fed, proof the secondaries are sums, not paint.
