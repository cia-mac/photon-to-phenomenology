The stimulus is drawn only as real luminance; the field anatomy (rings, labels, curve) is the cell's fixed wiring and never lights up with the stimulus. The meter is one integral of the on-screen light against the fixed difference-of-Gaussians weight map: center peak, surround dip, and full-field null all fall out of that single sum.
