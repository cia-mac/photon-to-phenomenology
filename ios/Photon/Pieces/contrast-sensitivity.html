Contrast sensitivity · the Campbell–Robson chart

Every bar in a given row has the same contrast, and every bar in a column the same width. Hold a relaxed gaze: the arc where the bars melt into flat grey is not printed on the screen, it is your own contrast sensitivity. Drop the overall contrast and watch the arc shrink; step back and the fine right edge washes out first.
