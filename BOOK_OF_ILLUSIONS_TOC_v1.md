# Ciamac's Book of Illusions

**Table of contents, v1, 2026-07-16.** Forty pieces. A proposal, not a decision.

The count is 40 by argument, not by ambition. Michael Bach's reference site holds
~141 illusions and ~71 interactive; the Best Illusion of the Year contest has ~210
finalists across 20 years but only a few dozen distinct mechanisms. The field's own
curators converge on several dozen genuinely distinct phenomena and a long tail of
restagings. Forty is the curated set where every page earns its place. Past ~60 you
are padding with variations, which is the trap the big collections fell into.

## The one test

**Does this catch your brain in the act?**

A piece that merely illustrates a fact is cut, however pretty. A piece where your
own visual system manufactures something that is provably not on the screen stays.
This is Article I of the Constitution, applied as a knife.

## The spine is the argument

The order is not Palmer's chapter order and this is not a companion to his book.
Palmer is the well, not the syllabus. The eight parts below are an argument that
builds: light arrives, the eye invents edges, color turns out to be in you, a flat
image becomes a world, motion appears where none exists, parts become wholes,
attention decides what exists at all, and the construction becomes your experience.
Read in order, the book proves its thesis. That spine, plus the register, is the
thing no existing collection has.

## Where you actually stand

**14 of 40 already exist.** That is the headline.

- **LIVE here (9):** Cornsweet, scintillating grid, afterimage, Müller-Lyer, Ponzo, Ebbinghaus, motion aftereffect, Kanizsa, café wall.
- **LIVE as films (2):** Mach bands (the staircase reveal), color constancy.
- **PORT in (3):** receptive field, contrast sensitivity, gestalt grouping. Per D-0071 they move from the gallery-stage sandbox into this repo under its chrome and standards.
- **CUT (1):** trichromatic mixing.
- **DEDUP (1):** the swarm's `opponent-afterimage` versus this repo's `afterimage`. Keep the stronger, retire the other. Do not ship both.

**26 to build.** At one piece a week, that is a two-season book. At the swarm's rate, faster, but the felt-effect gate is human-speed and that is the real constraint.

## Two rules this list forces

1. **Originality where the illusion is somebody's artwork.** The classical phenomena
   (Müller-Lyer, Ponzo, Kanizsa, Mach) are public scientific record; stage them
   freely. But the checker shadow is Adelson's and the famous drifting stagings are
   Kitaoka's. For those: build the phenomenon with your own stimulus, or credit and
   link to theirs. Never reproduce another artist's image as your own piece. Add
   this to the Constitution.
2. **The book is the argument, not the pile.** If it is 40 illusions, it competes
   with a free 141-illusion site and loses. If it is 40 illusions arranged as a
   proof that seeing is construction, in your register, it is a thing that does not
   exist. Every cut question is answered by "does the argument need this."
